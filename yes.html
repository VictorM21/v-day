"Isimi Mi" — My Rest, My Peace, My Heart ❤️

But distance has never been able to touch what I feel for you.

Every country, every city, every time zone — you are my constant.

No matter where I am in the world, Valentine's Day will always find me thinking of you. Not because of a date on the calendar, but because you are the reason my heart celebrates.

"Distance means so little when someone means so much."

— You are that someone, Isimi.

I built this website not just because it's Valentine's Day, but because I wanted you to feel me right beside you — even when I can't be. Every line of code, every heart animation, every Yoruba word, every photo we've taken together... it's all proof that you are never far from my heart.

When you love, you don't abandon your beloved.

Isimi, I am not abandoning you. Not today. Not ever. I am learning, growing, and building — so that one day, the distance will be a memory and every Valentine's Day will find us together, under the same sky, in the same home.

Until then, I will keep finding ways to reach you. A song. A letter. A website. A promise. Whatever it takes to make you feel as loved as you truly are.

Today, I promise you:

✅ Every Valentine's Day, I will make it special — near or far.

✅ Every year, I will love you more than the last.

✅ Every day, I am working toward our forever.

✅ You are my rest, and I will always come home to you.

No matter the distance. No matter the country.
You are my Valentine. Today. Always. Forever.

Yours, across every mile,
